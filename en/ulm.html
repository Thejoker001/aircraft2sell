Frequently asked questions

The questions light sport aircraft buyers and sellers ask most often.

What are the categories of light sport aircraft (ULM)?
French regulations define six classes: paramotor (class 1), weight-shift trike (class 2), three-axis (class 3), gyrocopter (class 4), airship/balloon (class 5) and ultralight helicopter (class 6). Most listings are three-axis aircraft and gyrocopters.
What budget should I plan for a used light sport aircraft?
From a few thousand euros for an older weight-shift trike to more than €100,000 for a recent, well-equipped three-axis aircraft. Engine hours, avionics, the ballistic parachute and general condition determine the price.
What should I check before buying?
The aircraft’s identification card and data sheet, engine hours (Rotax overhauls at 1,000 and 2,000 h), the ballistic parachute repack date, the maintenance logbook and compliance with weight limits.
Can a light sport aircraft bought abroad be flown in another country?
Usually yes, but national rules differ: the aircraft must be registered or identified with the local civil aviation authority and comply with local limits, notably maximum take-off weight. Check the requirements of your country before buying.
