Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about buying, selling and using Aircraft2Sell.

For buyers

How do I contact a seller?

On every listing page, click the "Contact seller" button. A form opens: enter your name, your email, your phone number (optional) and your message. The seller receives an email notification and can reply to you directly. No account is required to contact a seller.

Are listings verified?

Yes. Every listing is manually reviewed by our team before publication. We check the consistency of the information (category, price, description), but we do not carry out a physical inspection of the aircraft. We always recommend an independent pre-purchase inspection by an approved AMO before any commitment.

Does Aircraft2Sell take a commission on sales?

Zero commission. Aircraft2Sell is a matchmaking platform. We do not take any percentage on transactions: the full sale price goes to the seller. Our business model relies solely on seller subscriptions.

How do I create an alert to be notified of new listings?

On the home page, enter your email in the "Alerts" section, select a category and a maximum budget, then click "Create alert". You will receive an email as soon as a listing matching your criteria is published. You can unsubscribe with one click from each email you receive.

Can I save listings as favourites?

Yes. On listing cards, click the heart icon to add a listing to your favourites. Your favourites are saved in your browser and persist between visits. A free account lets you access them from all your devices.

Are prices negotiable?

Aircraft2Sell does not take part in negotiations: they happen entirely between you and the seller. Some listings state that the price is "negotiable" or "firm". Feel free to ask the seller directly about their room for negotiation, backing up your offer with objective elements (engine hours, condition, AVAC value).

For sellers

How do I list an aircraft?

Create a free account, then click "List an aircraft" in the navigation. Fill in the form (category, make, model, year, price, description, photos) and submit. Your listing is reviewed by our team within 24 to 72 business hours, then published automatically.

How many listings can I publish?

It depends on your plan: Essential (free): 1 active listing. Aviator (29 euros/month): 3 listings. Pro (79 euros/month): unlimited listings. You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time from your dashboard. See pricing.

Why wasn't my listing approved?

We reject listings that do not comply with our rules: incomplete or inaccurate information, insufficient or misleading photos, price inconsistent with the category, duplicate listing. You receive an email explaining the reason for rejection and you can correct it and resubmit from your dashboard.

How many photos can I add?

Essential plan: up to 5 photos. Aviator and Pro plans: up to 20 photos. We recommend at least 6 photos covering the exterior (front 3/4, rear 3/4, profile), the cockpit, the engine and the documents. High-quality photos noticeably increase the number of contacts received.

How do I edit or delete my listing?

From your dashboard ("My listings" tab), click the listing you want to edit. You can edit all the information, add or remove photos, archive or delete the listing. If the aircraft is sold, mark it as "Sold" rather than deleting the listing: this strengthens the platform's credibility.

Who sees my email address and phone number?

Your email is never publicly displayed on listings. Only your public username is visible. Your email is passed on to buyers only when they contact you via the form, so you can reply directly. Your phone number is only shared if you choose to include it in your listing.

Account and subscription

Is creating an account free?

Yes, entirely free. The Essential plan lets you publish 1 listing, receive messages from buyers and access your dashboard. No credit card is required to sign up.

How do I cancel my subscription?

From your dashboard, "Subscription" tab, click "Cancel my subscription". Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period: you keep access to all features until the end of the paid period. No minimum notice is required.

How do I change my username?

In your dashboard, "Settings" tab, edit your username in the dedicated field and click "Save". The change takes effect immediately on your profile, but listings already published keep the old username until their next update.

I forgot my password, what should I do?

On the login page, click "Forgot your password?". Enter your email: you will receive a reset link valid for 1 hour. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or write to us at contact@aircraft2sell.eu.

Technical questions

Does the website work on mobile?

Yes. Aircraft2Sell is fully responsive and optimised for mobile and tablet. All features (search, filters, contacting sellers, listing an aircraft) are accessible from a smartphone. Uploading photos from your phone's gallery is supported.

What photo formats are accepted?

Accepted formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG. Maximum size: 10 MB per photo. We recommend horizontal photos in 4:3 or 16:9 format, with a minimum resolution of 1280x720 pixels. Blurry, too dark, or very distant photos are rejected during review.

Is the currency converter reliable?

The converter uses exchange rates from public APIs (indicative rates close to the European Central Bank, updated regularly). These rates are indicative: for a transaction, always use the rate from your bank or a specialist currency exchange service.

Legal and security

Does Aircraft2Sell display advertising?

No. Aircraft2Sell is entirely ad-free. Our business model relies on seller subscriptions: we never display banners, pop-ups or sponsored content.

How do I report a suspicious listing?

On every listing page, click the "Report" link at the bottom of the page. You can also write to us at contact@aircraft2sell.eu with the listing link and the reason for the report. We handle all reports within 24 hours.

Is my personal data protected?

Yes. Aircraft2Sell complies with GDPR. Your data is hosted on secure servers (Supabase / Cloudflare) and is never sold to third parties. You can exercise your rights (access, rectification, deletion) at any time. See our privacy policy.
