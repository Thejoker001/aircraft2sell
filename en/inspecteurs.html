Pre-Purchase Inspector Directory

Before buying a used aircraft, have its actual condition checked by an independent professional — an approved mechanic, EASA expert or maintenance workshop.

Why a pre-purchase inspection?

On a marketplace between private individuals, the buyer has no guarantee of the aircraft's actual condition before paying. An independent inspection — with no connection to the seller — checks the engine condition, the airframe, the airworthiness documents and the maintenance history before you commit.

What an inspector checks

Flight and engine logbooks, compliance with airworthiness directives (AD/SB), condition of the airframe and flight controls, avionics, corrosion, and consistency between the displayed hours and the documents.

A budget before the purchase, not after

An inspection generally costs a fraction of the aircraft's price — well worth it if it prevents buying a hidden problem. It is negotiated directly with the inspector, outside Aircraft2Sell's (zero) commission.
