Frequently asked questions

The questions airliner buyers and sellers ask most often.

Who buys used airliners?
Regional and low-cost airlines, leasing companies, cargo operators, passenger-to-freighter (P2F) conversion companies and teardown companies for parts resale.
Which technical data is expected in a listing?
Airframe hours and cycles, engine type and status (hours and cycles since last shop visit, remaining life-limited parts), heavy-check status (C-check, D-check), cabin configuration, and ownership and operating history.
Do transactions go through Aircraft2Sell?
No. The platform connects seller and buyer; negotiation, inspection, letter of intent and sale agreement are handled directly between the parties, with no commission taken by Aircraft2Sell.
Can I list several aircraft from the same account?
Yes. Airlines, lessors and brokers post multiple listings from a professional account, with promotion options. See the pricing page.
