Aviation Market Trends

Average prices observed by category, calculated in real time from listings published on Aircraft2Sell. Indicative data, not a professional appraisal.

Methodology. These figures are calculated solely from active listings published on Aircraft2Sell (no archive of completed sales, no external data). A category only displays an average price if it has at least 3 active listings — below that, the sample is too small to be representative. Prices in foreign currency are converted to euros at the rate shown on the site; prices marked "on request" are excluded from the calculation.
