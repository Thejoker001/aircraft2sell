Frequently asked questions

The questions business jet buyers and sellers ask most often.

Which categories of business jets are listed on Aircraft2Sell?
From very light jets (Phenom 100, Citation Mustang) to light jets (Citation CJ, Phenom 300), midsize (Citation XLS, Learjet 60), super-midsize and long-range aircraft (Falcon, Gulfstream, Global). Listings come from private owners, dealers, brokers and operators.
Which technical information should I look at first?
Airframe hours and cycles, engine status and whether they are enrolled in an hourly maintenance programme, RVSM and ADS-B Out compliance, heavy-check history, cabin configuration and the status of current maintenance programmes.
Is a pre-purchase inspection necessary for a jet?
Yes, it is standard practice: an inspection at a Part-145 approved maintenance facility, together with a full records review, is carried out before the sale agreement is signed.
Can professionals (brokers, operators) post listings?
Yes. Aircraft2Sell is open to private individuals and professionals alike: dealers, brokers, operators and management companies post from a professional account, with promotion options. See the pricing page.
