Frequently asked questions from professionals

Can a dealer manage several listings at the same time?
Yes. The Aviator (3 listings) and Pro (unlimited listings) plans are designed for sellers who manage several aircraft simultaneously, with a centralised dashboard to track views, contacts and the status of each listing.
Does Aircraft2Sell take a commission if I sell through the platform?
No. The business model relies solely on a fixed monthly subscription, never on a percentage of the sale price. That's the main difference from most brokers and competing platforms.
How are my listings promoted to professional buyers?
Dedicated categories (business jets, turboprops, airliners) and search filters by budget, country and equipment direct qualified buyers straight to your listings, with no extra intermediary.
Can I import my existing fleet?
Yes. Send us your list of aircraft via the form above: we'll help you create the listings and check each one before publication.
