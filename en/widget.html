Listings widget for your website

Embed your Aircraft2Sell listings on your flying club's or brokerage's website, for free.

A simple widget for partners

Flying clubs, brokers and resellers: display your Aircraft2Sell listings directly on your own website. The widget updates automatically every time a listing is published, with no maintenance required on your end.

  • Enter the email of the seller account that publishes your listings on Aircraft2Sell.
  • Click "Generate code", then copy the code shown.
  • Paste it into your website's HTML, wherever you want your listings to appear.

The widget displays the seller's 10 most recent live listings: photo, model, year and price, with a link to the full listing page on aircraft2sell.eu. Free to use for Aircraft2Sell partners.
