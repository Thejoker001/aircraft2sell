How long does it take to sell an aircraft in Europe

Factors that influence how long it takes to sell an aircraft, and tips to sell faster.

Average selling time observed on the market

In Europe, a well-maintained light aircraft priced correctly generally sells within 2 to 6 months. Business jets and turboprops, a smaller market with more demanding buyers, can take 3 to 12 months. An overpriced aircraft or one with an incomplete file can stay on the market for over a year.

Factors that influence the selling time

  • Price — by far the number one factor. A price aligned with the market often cuts the selling time in half.
  • Overall condition and maintenance — a complete, up-to-date file immediately reassures serious buyers.
  • How rare the model is — a highly sought-after type sells faster than a niche model, even if well maintained.
  • Listing quality — photos, a detailed description and responsiveness to messages significantly speed up buyer contact.
  • Season — the market is generally more active in spring and autumn than in the depths of winter.

Price, the top lever to speed things up

A serious market study before setting your price remains the best time investment you can make. Compare your aircraft with listings that have actually sold recently (not just still-active listings, which can be overpriced) for similar models, years and flight hours.

Word of caution — A price set too high at the start, even if corrected later, leaves a trace: serious buyers track the market and are wary of a listing that has been lingering for months.

A complete listing sells faster

A listing with at least 10 quality photos, a detailed description (airframe and engine hours, equipment, maintenance history) and a clearly displayed price generates significantly more contacts than a minimal listing with "price on request".

Tips to reduce the selling time

  • Prepare the complete file (logbooks, maintenance invoices, applicable airworthiness directives) before you even publish the listing.
  • Respond quickly to messages: a buyer who gets no reply within 24 to 48 hours often turns to another listing.
  • Offer an easy inspection: a seller who readily accepts an independent expertise reassures buyers and speeds up their decision.
  • Adjust the price if no serious viewing has taken place after 4 to 6 weeks of publication.
Tip — Publish on a platform that reaches a broad European audience: the more your listing is seen by qualified buyers, the faster it sells.
