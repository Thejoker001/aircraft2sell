Frequently asked questions

The questions light aircraft buyers and sellers ask most often.

What budget should I plan for a used light aircraft?
An older Cessna 150/152 or Robin DR400 can be found from a few tens of thousands of euros. A recent Cessna 172 or Piper PA-28, a Diamond DA40 or a Cirrus SR22 sell for considerably more. Price mainly depends on airframe hours, remaining engine potential (SMOH), avionics and maintenance history.
Which documents should I check before buying?
A valid certificate of airworthiness and airworthiness review certificate (ARC), the airframe, engine and propeller logbooks, the inspection history and applied airworthiness directives, and the status of life-limited components. A pre-purchase inspection by an independent workshop is strongly recommended.
Can I buy an aircraft registered in another European country?
Yes. Transferring registration between EASA member states is a routine procedure: deregistration in the country of origin, then new registration and ARC in the receiving country. Allow for administrative lead times and associated fees.
