Questions about pricing

Is there a commission on sales?
No. Aircraft2Sell does not take any commission. You only pay your monthly or annual plan and keep 100% of your aircraft's sale price.
Can I change plan?
Yes, at any time from your account. The change takes effect immediately and the balance is calculated pro rata.
How long does my listing stay visible?
Essential listings are visible for 60 days. Aviator and Pro listings are visible for 30 days and renew automatically as long as your subscription is active.
How does annual billing work?
By choosing annual billing, you save 20% compared to the monthly rate: Aviator works out to 278 € per year instead of 348 €, and Pro to 758 € per year instead of 948 €.
What types of aircraft can I publish?
All civil aircraft: light aircraft, turboprops, business jets, helicopters, microlights, gliders, airliners and spare parts. Active military aircraft are not accepted.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, with no commitment, from your account. Your listings remain visible until the end of the current period.

Zero commission, whatever your plan

A 5 M€ jet or a 20,000 € microlight: you keep the full sale price. Aircraft2Sell earns revenue solely through subscriptions.
