The general aviation market in France

The French general aviation market is one of the most active in Europe. Regulation falls under the DGAC for national matters and EASA for the airworthiness of certified aircraft: a used aircraft must remain under a valid certificate of airworthiness, with an up-to-date ARC and complete compliance with airworthiness directives. F- registrations are issued by the DGAC after de-registration in the country of origin, a common procedure for aircraft imported from another European state.

On the tax side, the sale of a used aircraft between private individuals is generally exempt from VAT, while a purchase from a professional may fall under the margin scheme. It is advisable to clarify this point before signing. Flying clubs, often affiliated with the FFA, form the core of demand: Cessna 172, Piper PA-28, Robin DR400 and TB10 are favoured there for training and touring. Flight schools regularly renew their fleets, creating a steady flow of listings.

The current trend favours well-equipped aircraft: modern avionics, mode S transponders, ADS-B and LED lighting are decisive arguments on resale. Buyers carefully check engine hours since overhaul (SMOH), inspection history and complete documentation. Frequently asked questions

What buyers of aircraft in France most often ask.

What are the steps to register a used aircraft purchased in France?
De-registration is carried out in the country of origin, then the DGAC issues an F- registration upon presentation of the complete file: proof of ownership, certificate of airworthiness and evidence of airworthiness directive compliance. The Airworthiness Review Certificate (ARC) must be valid. Expect a few weeks of administrative delay.
Does VAT apply to the purchase of a used aircraft in France?
A sale between private individuals is generally not subject to VAT. However, a purchase from a professional may be subject to VAT or fall under the margin scheme. It is advisable to have the seller clarify the tax regime before the transaction.
Can a foreign pilot buy an aircraft in France?
Yes. The aircraft can be registered in France if the buyer has their main residence there, or in their home country. EASA rules make it easier to transfer registration between member states, with de-registration and re-registration to plan for.
