Before you write to us

Answers to the most frequently asked questions.

Full FAQ
How do I post a listing?
Create a free account, click "Post a listing" and fill in the form. Your listing is approved by our team within 24 hours.
Are there commissions on sales?
No, zero commission. You only pay for your listing plan and keep 100% of the sale price.
How are sellers verified?
Every professional seller goes through a manual check: identity, aviation certification and legal documents are verified before publication.
Which countries is the site active in?
Aircraft2Sell is active in 32 countries across Europe. Sellers and buyers from the entire ICAO Europe region can use the platform.
How do I contact a seller?
Open the listing page, click "Contact seller" and send your message. The seller receives your request directly by email.
Can I edit my listing after publication?
Yes, from your dashboard you can edit the text, photos and price at any time. Significant changes may require a new review.
