Frequently asked questions

The questions helicopter buyers and sellers ask most often.

Piston or turbine helicopter?
The Robinson R22 and R44 (piston) offer the most affordable entry point for training and leisure flying. The R66, Airbus H125, Bell 407 or Leonardo AW109 (turbine) bring performance, reliability and capacity for aerial work and passenger transport.
What is remaining component life and why does it matter so much?
Many helicopter components (blades, hubs, gearboxes) have a limited life in hours or years. The price depends directly on the hours remaining before replacement or overhaul — for example the 12-year / 2,200-hour overhaul on Robinson helicopters.
Which documents should I ask the seller for?
Airframe and engine logbooks, life-limited component tracking records, the history of applied airworthiness directives, a valid ARC and the operating history (school, aerial work, private).
Can I post a listing as an operator or flight school?
Yes. Operators, flight schools and aerial work companies post from a professional account, with no commission on the sale. See the pricing page.
