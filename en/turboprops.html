Frequently asked questions

The questions turboprop buyers and sellers ask most often.

Why choose a turboprop over a jet?
Lower operating costs, the ability to use short or unpaved runways and a good payload. Turboprops are particularly well suited to regional trips of 300 to 1,500 km.
Single-engine or twin-engine?
The PC-12 and TBM family deliver high performance on a single engine, and commercial single-engine IFR operations (SET-IMC) are now permitted in Europe. The King Air remains the twin-engine benchmark for those who prioritise redundancy.
Which technical points should I check before buying?
Airframe hours and cycles, engine time since overhaul and since hot section inspection (HSI), enrolment in a maintenance programme, propeller and pressurisation condition, and the generation of avionics installed.
How can I sell my turboprop quickly?
A complete sheet, quality photos, up-to-date records and a price in line with the market make the difference. Your listing is visible in more than 30 countries, with no commission. Our valuation tool helps you set the price.
