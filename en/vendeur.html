Frequently asked questions

The answers to the questions sellers ask before publishing.

Full FAQ
How much does it cost to post a listing?

The Essential plan is free: one active listing with 5 photos, visible for 60 days. The Aviator and Pro plans add listings, photos and visibility. In all cases, no commission is charged on the sale.

When does my listing go live?

Our team reviews every listing manually. It is published within 24 hours, 48 hours at most. You are notified by email.

Who can sell on Aircraft2Sell?

Both private individuals and professionals: owners, flying clubs, brokers and dealers, across the 32 European countries covered.

Are my contact details visible?

No. Buyers contact you through the built-in messaging. You decide who you share your phone number or email with.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

Yes, at any time from your dashboard: update the price, photos, mark as "sold" or delete.
